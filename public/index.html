Frequently asked questions

When do the celebrations start?

The traditional marriage is Thursday, 10 December 2026, and the holy matrimony is Saturday, 12 December 2026. Both start at 10:00 a.m. WAT. See the schedule.

Where is the wedding venue?

The holy matrimony is at The Golden Heart Place in Akai-Effa, Calabar. The traditional marriage takes place at The Bride Family House in Umuosi Lagwa Okwuato, Aboh Mbaise. Open the Calabar area map.

What should I wear?

Choose something elegant and comfortable. Deep purple, lilac, emerald green, and army green are our wedding colours.

Where can I look for a place to stay?

The accommodation section has map searches near both ceremony locations.
